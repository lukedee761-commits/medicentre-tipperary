The doctor

Dr Bastiaan van Eynatten

Some doctors treat the complaint in front of them. Dr van Eynatten is known around Tipperary for something slower and rarer — looking at your issue in the whole: your body, your history, your mind, your home life, and only then the prescription pad.

Before Tipperary, he practised in Cork, and today he runs surgeries in both Tipperary Town and Bansha as a member of the Rural, Island and Dispensing Doctors of Ireland. His special interests are GP-level dermatology and fertility medicine.

You won’t be a stranger here for long. Nurse Anna keeps the clinical side humming, and Mairéad, Agnes and Richella at reception will likely know your name by the second visit.

Common questions

Do you take medical cards?

Yes. The practice holds a GMS contract at both Tipperary Town and Bansha — medical card and GP visit card patients are welcome, alongside private patients.

Are you taking new patients?

Call us on 062 52655 and we’ll talk it through — it’s the quickest way to a straight answer.

What happens outside opening hours?

Urgent care out of hours is provided by Caredoc on 0818 300 365. In an emergency, always call 999 or 112.

How do I book an appointment?

Ring 062 52655 during opening hours. Reception will find a time that suits — and if it’s about something sensitive, just say so and they’ll handle it quietly.
